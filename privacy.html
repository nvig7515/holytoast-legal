Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Holy Toast LLC ("Holy Toast," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the Holy Toast app for iPhone and Android (the "App"), order from us for pickup or delivery, or take part in the Holy Club loyalty program and community. It also explains the choices and rights you have.

Holy Toast LLC is a Florida limited liability company. Our mailing address is 9520 Harding Ave, Surfside, FL 33154, and you can reach us about privacy at accounting@holytoast.com.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information. Your name and the details of the sign-in method you choose: a mobile phone number (verified by a one-time text message code), an email address and password, Sign in with Apple, or Sign in with Google. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an identifier from Apple or Google and, if you allow it, your name and email address. We never receive your Apple or Google password. If you use an email and password, we store only a one-way hash of the password, never the password itself.
  • Profile information. An optional profile photo.
  • Orders. The items you order, choices and special instructions, the store, whether the order is for pickup or delivery, the tip, the totals, and any points you redeem. If you change an order before the kitchen starts it, we record the change.
  • Delivery information. For delivery orders, the delivery address, any delivery notes you add (for example a gate code), and the phone number the driver can use to reach you. You can save addresses to your profile under names you choose, such as "Home" or "Work."
  • Payment information. Card and wallet payments (including Apple Pay and Google Pay) are entered into and processed by our payment processor, Stripe. Your full card number and security code go to Stripe, not to us. We receive a payment reference, the amount, and the result of the payment.
  • Community content. Photos and captions you post, comments, likes, the members you follow, and reports you submit about posts or members. Posts are reviewed by our team before they appear in the community.
  • Ratings and problem reports. Star ratings, comments and problem reports you submit about an order.
  • Holy Club activity. Points earned and redeemed, points you send to or receive from other members along with any note you add, referral codes, coupon codes, and streak and reward history.
  • Messages to us. Anything you send us by email or through the App.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information. Your device platform (iOS or Android), app version, and a push notification token if you allow notifications.
  • Server logs. When the App talks to our servers, we record technical details of the request, such as IP address, time, and the address of the request. We use these logs to run the service, fix problems and prevent abuse.
  • Delivery tracking. While a delivery is in progress, our delivery partner sends us the driver's status, estimated arrival time, approximate location, and, when the driver takes one, a photo of where the order was left.

Location

If you allow location access, the App uses your approximate location on your device to show the nearest Holy Toast location. That location is not sent to our servers and we do not track your location. You can turn location access off at any time in your device settings, and the App will still work.

When you type a delivery address, the text you enter is sent to the address-search service built into your device (Apple Maps on iPhone, the Google location service on Android) to suggest matching addresses.

Information we do not collect

We do not access your contacts, we do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites. The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics tools.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and verify your sign-in.
  • To take, prepare, charge for and hand off your orders, and to arrange delivery through our delivery partner.
  • To show live order and delivery status and send you order notifications.
  • To run the Holy Club: points, tiers, rewards, streaks, referrals, coupons and promotions.
  • To run the community: showing posts, likes, comments and profiles, ordering the feed (including showing posts from members you follow first), and reviewing posts and reports.
  • To respond to you, including about ratings and problem reports, refunds and support requests.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, for example repeated sign-up bonuses or points abuse, and to keep the service secure.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

3. What other members can see

The Holy Club community is visible to other signed-in members. If you take part, other members can see:

  • your display name, profile photo, Holy Club tier, and the month you joined;
  • your approved posts and captions, the comments you write, and the posts you like (each post shows who liked it);
  • how many members you follow and how many follow you, and those lists.

When you send points to another member, that member sees your name and any note you add. Your phone number, email address, delivery addresses and order history are never shown to other members.

You can block a member at any time. Blocked members cannot see your profile or posts, and you will not see theirs. You can report a post or a member, and our team reviews every report.

5. Text messages

If you sign in with a phone number, you agree to receive text messages from Holy Toast with one-time verification codes. One message is sent each time you request a code. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop and HELP for help, or contact us at accounting@holytoast.com. We do not send marketing text messages, and we do not share your phone number with anyone for their marketing.

For delivery orders, the driver may contact you about that delivery by phone or text through our delivery partner.

7. How we protect information

The App connects to our servers only over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are stored as one-way bcrypt hashes. Card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored on our servers. Access to customer information is limited to staff and service providers who need it to do their work. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by Florida law (Fla. Stat. § 501.171) and any other applicable law.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account. In the App, go to Profile, then Delete Account. You can also email accounting@holytoast.com from the email or phone on your account. When you delete your account, we remove your name, phone number, email, sign-in identifiers, profile photo, saved addresses, community posts and their photos, comments, likes, follows and blocks right away. Order and points records are kept without those identifiers as described in Section 6.
  • Access and correction. You can change your name, photo and saved addresses in the App. To request a copy of your information or a correction, email us. We will respond within 45 days and may need to confirm that the request comes from you.
  • Notifications. You can turn push notifications off in your device settings.
  • Location. You can turn location access off in your device settings.
  • Community. You can delete your posts and comments, unfollow or block members, and report content at any time.

State privacy rights. Depending on where you live, state law may give you additional rights, such as the right to know what personal information we have collected, to get a copy of it, to have it corrected or deleted, and to opt out of the sale of personal information or its use for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. To make a request, email accounting@holytoast.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject. We will not treat you differently for making a request. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our response.

Outside the United States. Holy Toast operates in the United States and stores information on servers in the United States. If you use the App from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

9. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us at accounting@holytoast.com and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App and our practices change. We will post the updated policy here with a new effective date. If we make a material change, we will tell you in the App or by email before it takes effect.
